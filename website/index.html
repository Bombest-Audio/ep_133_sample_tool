02 / WHAT IT DOES

Small tool. Sharp edges.

Everything stays on your machine. We don't want your data. We want you making beats.

F·01

Offline-first

Runs with the wifi off. Your samples never touch a server. The only thing it ever fetches is TE's public firmware list, to flag if your KO-II is out of date.

F·02

Projects-only backup

Back up and restore whole projects. Move a kit between units, or roll back a bad save.

F·03

Colors & bank names

Rename banks and recolor pads so a glance tells you where the kick lives.

F·04 · THE BIG ONE

Import your own WAVs over USB

Push your own samples straight onto the device. We reverse-engineered the file protocol to make this work. It's the whole reason this tool exists.

SYSEX UPLOAD
F·05

Serial-number stripping

Backups come out clean. Share a project without leaking your unit's serial.

F·06

No telemetry. Ever.

No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting. Read the source and check for yourself.
